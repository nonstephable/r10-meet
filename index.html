Why Mercado Pago is the bridge
✅ Connects to Zak natively
Mercado Pago has a built-in integration with Zak by WuBook. When a guest checks out and pays via Mercado Pago, Zak automatically sends the checkout data through.
en.wubook.net · Verified May 2026
✅ Connects to Alegra natively
Mercado Pago also integrates directly with Alegra. So when the data comes in from Zak, Alegra receives it and generates the DIAN-compliant invoice automatically.
alegra.com · Verified May 2026
Mercado Pago also has a physical card reader
The Mercado Pago Point device works just like a datáfono same experience for guests paying by card in person. The difference is what happens after the payment goes through.

Key things we uncovered

Juan confirmed the payment split at the hostel. That means the majority of checkouts are already eligible for full automation. The manual step is not a small problem it's happening on most transactions, every day.
✅ Student housing is already automated
Long-stay students use a fixed monthly billing setup that runs automatically. The friction we're solving is specific to the hostel side which is exactly the right place to start.
🔍 Redeban is in the picture
The student housing side uses Redeban not Bold connected directly to the bank account. This is useful context: R10 already has experience with more than one payment provider, which makes the conversation about switching on the hostel side more familiar territory.

Juan shared that Zak has let them down before on API promises things that looked possible on paper didn't deliver in practice. This is worth taking seriously. Confirming the Mercado Pago connection works end-to-end before committing to anything is the right call.

Juan hasn't seen Mercado Pago's physical reader (Point device) in use locally. Before recommending it as a Bold replacement for in-person payments, we need to confirm it's readily available and functional in Bogotá.
